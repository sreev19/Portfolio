Choosing the Right Hypothesis Test: A Case Study and Simulation Comparison of Parametric, Exact and Non-Parametric Tests

A comparison of 15 hypothesis tests (Z, t, Welch t, paired t, ANOVA, F, chi-square, Fisher exact, Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon, Kruskal-Wallis, sign test and others) on a case study of three Maths teaching methods, stress-tested with simulation.

10,000Monte Carlo replications per scenario (NumPy, SciPy), measuring Type I error and power
12.3% vs 5%false-positive rate of the pooled t test vs the Welch t test when variances and group sizes differ
+19 pppower gained by rank-based tests on non-normal data

Scenarios covered normal, unequal-variance, skewed, heavy-tailed and outlier-contaminated data. The findings became a scenario-based decision guide and flowchart, supported by 9 Matplotlib figures.
